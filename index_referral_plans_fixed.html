TERMS & CONDITIONS

Terms & risk disclosure

Rules for using the service, deposits, plans, referrals and withdrawals.

Risk notice: Trading involves a risk of loss. No profit or daily return is guaranteed. Market conditions can reduce or eliminate the value of deposited funds.

1. Trading and market risk

Deposited funds may be used in live market trading. Digital asset prices can move rapidly, and you may lose part or all of your capital. Past performance does not predict future results.

2. Plans and term

Each plan has a 10-day term. The plan amount represents allocated capital and does not guarantee profit, interest, commission or return of principal. Each plan is tracked separately and summarized in your account.

3. Profit calculation and claims

Profit is not generated at a fixed or guaranteed rate. Only positive trading results verified and recorded by the administrator become eligible for a claim. A user may claim available verified profit at any time after it has been recorded. Live projections are illustrative only and are not payable or withdrawable funds.

4. Deposits

The minimum deposit is 0.10 USDT. Deposits below this amount are not credited. Only the designated BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20) network is supported. Assets sent to the wrong network or address may not be recoverable.

5. Withdrawals

The minimum withdrawal is 0.30 USDT. Requests are subject to review and manual processing. Make sure the wallet address is correct before submitting a request.

6. Referrals and commission

When an invited user purchases their first eligible plan, the referring account receives a one-time 0.05 USDT commission after server verification. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts and fraudulent activity are not eligible. Commission can be used for an eligible plan purchase or withdrawn when the minimum withdrawal requirement is met.

7. Balances and records

Account balances and activity are based on server records. Network delays, invalid transaction hashes, verification failures or technical issues may delay updates. The server record is authoritative for deposits, plan purchases, commissions and withdrawals.

8. User responsibilities

Understand the risks, fees, limits and terms before using the service. Do not use borrowed funds or funds you cannot afford to lose. You are responsible for complying with the laws and financial regulations applicable in your location.

9. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated for security, legal or operational reasons. Continued use of the service after updated terms are published constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
